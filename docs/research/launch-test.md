# Timed launch test — kit

**Goal (Sprint 7.6):** prove that a non-technical owner launches a premium website from a WURK template in under an hour, without help.

**Pass:** median time to launch under 60 minutes **and** every participant launches without help. Anything less: we fix what slowed people down and test again.

---

## 1. Who to recruit (at least 5)

- **Real small-business owners** (or the person who'd run the website) — not WURK staff, developers or designers.
- **Non-technical:** comfortable with WhatsApp and email; has never built a website.
- **A spread of categories** — e.g. a salon, a dental or doctor's practice, a mechanic, a restaurant or guesthouse, a tradesperson.
- **A spread of devices:** at least two on a **phone**, at least two on a **laptop**.
- They bring: their **logo** (or not — that's realistic), **5–10 photos** of their business on their device, their **services and prices**, **opening hours** and **contact details**.

Offer a thank-you (e.g. three months free). Don't tell them the time goal — it changes behaviour.

## 2. Consent (POPIA)

Before starting, read this and get a clear **yes** (recorded or written):

> "We're testing WURK, not you. We'd like to watch your screen and record it so we can improve the product. The recording is only used by the WURK team for this purpose and is deleted within 90 days. You can stop at any time. Your business details stay yours — you can delete the test website afterwards or keep it. Is that okay?"

Record screen only where possible (not their face). Store recordings with restricted access; delete them on schedule.

## 3. The session (about 75 minutes)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 5 min | Welcome, consent, "think aloud" — say what you're looking for and what you expect |
| ≤ 60 min | The task (below). **Don't help.** If they're stuck for 3 minutes, note it, then ask "what would you do now?" — still don't show them |
| 10 min | Debrief: "What was hardest? What surprised you? Would you use this for your business?" |

**The task, read aloud once:** "Please create a website for your business on WURK and publish it, the way you'd want customers to see it. Tell us when you're happy with it."

**Timing:** WURK records it automatically — **Platform → Time to launch** shows each business's minutes from creating the business to first publishing. Also note the start time of signup yourself (signup itself isn't in WURK's figure).

**"Without help"** means the facilitator never explains, points or clicks. A participant who needs help to continue counts as **not succeeding** (we still let them finish, with help, to learn the rest).

## 5. After the sessions

1. Put every stuck point in one list, with how many participants hit it.
2. Fix anything **two or more** people hit, and anything that **stopped** someone, first.
3. Re-test with at least two **new** participants.
4. Record results in docs/sprints/sprint-07.md (progress log, 7.6) — median, success rate, what changed.
