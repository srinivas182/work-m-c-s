# Sprint 13 — Referral engine (approved 3 Nov 2026)

**Plan (sprint-plan.md):** self + 7 levels; pending/available; wallet rules; attribution; customer payouts; My WURK.
**PRD:** §7.5 Referral programme, §7.6 Record sale, §9 Customers and My WURK; acceptance criteria 6–7.

## Findings before planning

1. **Record sale (PRD §7.6) and customer accounts/portal (PRD §9) don't exist yet**, and no earlier sprint built them — but commission triggers on Record sale, and customers need somewhere to see their link, wallet and payouts. Both are included below, so Sprint 13 is larger than its outline: **nine parts**, released together.
2. **Legal:** the PRD's risk register requires a **legal opinion on referral programmes before launch** (Consumer Protection Act; multi-level commission). See decision A.
3. **Already in place:** package guardrails (`referral_max_levels`, `referral_max_commission_bp`); contacts (12.1) as the one record per customer; payments, refunds and the message templates (12.5) that new messages join.

## Parts

| Part | What |
|---|---|
| **13.1 Programme rules** | Settings → Referrals (owners): on/off (**off by default**); self-cashback plus up to the package's levels (default max 7), each a % — **the total can't exceed the package's cap**; attribution **first referrer for life** or **last referrer**; how long a link counts (days); the **return window** (days) before commission becomes available. No percentages are preset — the owner chooses. |
| **13.2 Links and attribution** | Every customer (contact) gets a referral code and link (`?ref=CODE` on any page of the site) and a downloadable QR code. A visit with a code remembers the referrer (first-party, for the link's validity) and attaches it when the person becomes a customer. Self-referral and loops are refused; one referrer per customer per business; changes logged. |
| **13.3 Commission ledger** | When a payment is confirmed — **online orders, bookings paid online, Record sale** — commission is written for the buyer (self-cashback) and up to N levels of referrers, as **pending**; after the return window it becomes **available**; a refund **reverses** it (partial refunds proportionally); the package cap is enforced. Every entry is an immutable ledger line (amount, level, source, status changes with who/when). |
| **13.4 Wallets** | One wallet per customer per business (never shared between businesses): available balance from the ledger. **Wallet rules** (owner): payouts allowed or not, minimum payout, maximum % of an order payable by wallet, balance expiry (months; expiry logged). **Spending:** at shop checkout and online booking payments (decision C), within the rules. |
| **13.5 Record sale (in-store)** | Staff screen (phone-friendly): find the customer (phone, or scan their QR) → amount → cash/card/EFT → optional link to a booking → save. Creates the contact if new; commission as above; the customer gets a confirmation (message templates). **Fraud controls:** audit with staff and device; **manager approval above an owner-set amount**; the pending period. Permission `sales.record`; approval `sales.approve`. |
| **13.6 Customer accounts and portal** | On the business's own site (`/account`): sign in by **one-time code** to phone or email — always available — or, if the business chooses (decision B), by **password**: new customers receive a **default password they must change** (generated by WURK, sent once, never seen by staff, changed at first sign-in, expires unused after 7 days); customers with an existing WURK ID keep their own. Shows: referral link and QR, the people they referred and their activity (first name only), commissions (pending/available), wallet and expiry, payout requests, orders and bookings. POPIA consent on first sign-in; data access and deletion requests. |
| **13.7 Payouts** | Customer requests a payout (if allowed, above the minimum) → owner approves or declines → pays from the business's own funds → marks paid with a reference. Messages (templates): commission earned, commission available, payout paid. |
| **13.8 My WURK** | `my.wurk.co.za`: one sign-in (WURK ID) listing every business the customer belongs to, with one-tap sign-in to each business's portal. **Business A never learns about Business B** (isolation tests). |
| **13.9 Reports** | Owner: top referrers, referral sales vs commission cost, outstanding wallet liability (pending and available), payouts. Dashboard tiles: referral sales, wallet liability. Inter-branch settlement (11.5) records wallet earned at one branch and spent at another. |
| **Tests** | Commission for self + 7 levels across orders, paid bookings and Record sale; caps; pending → available; refunds (full/partial) reverse; first vs last attribution; link validity; loops refused; wallet rules (max %, minimum payout, expiry); approval threshold; isolation (no data between businesses; My WURK shows only the customer's own); accessibility on desktop and phone. |

## Decisions for 13.4 (WURK, 4 Nov 2026)

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D | **No refunds after the return window closes.** The window is the business's own ("Refunds and returns: N days", Settings → Payments; default 14; 0 = no refunds) and applies whether or not it runs referrals. Orders count from payment; bookings and class deposits from the appointment (refunds there follow the cancellation policy, before the appointment). Referral rewards become available when it closes — so a refund can never touch an available reward, wallets never go negative and nobody owes money back. A refund made directly in a payment provider's dashboard can't be stopped by WURK; rewards stay final. With 0 days, deposits aren't refunded on cancellation either. |
| E | **Rewards on the full order**, including any part paid from the wallet. |
| F | **Refunds of orders paid partly from the wallet:** the wallet part back to the wallet; the rest back to the card or account. |

## 13.6e Data requests (POPIA) — specification and decisions (WURK, 6 Nov 2026)

**Customers (portal → Your information):** *Download a copy* — instantly, everything the business holds about them: details, full history (orders, bookings, classes, in-store sales, their own form enquiries and chats), referral code, people referred (first names), every reward and wallet movement, consent date. *Ask for deletion* — records a request; the customer sees its status.

**Businesses (Customers → Data requests; `contacts.manage`):** every request, its age and actions. Approving, in order: (1) the **wallet balance is settled first** — paid out or refunded and recorded (until 13.7, the owner records how: EFT with a reference, or cash; the same "paid out" ledger entry 13.7 uses); (2) the customer record is **anonymised** (name, phone, email removed; marketing consent cleared); (3) portal access ends and the referral link stops working; (4) **pending rewards are forfeited** (not yet earned and still refundable — waiting would delay deletion by weeks; the customer is told before confirming); (5) orders, sales, bookings and patient records are **kept as the law requires** (tax 5 years, patient records 6) and the reply says so; (6) the customer is emailed the outcome while their address is still known. Declining needs a reason, emailed to the customer.

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| G | Approving a deletion pays out or refunds the wallet balance first (WURK, option 2). |
| H | Every step is in the audit log (WURK) — request, wallet settled, approved/declined, who and when — **without** the personal details being deleted (record ids, amounts and who acted only). |
| I | Pending rewards are forfeited on deletion (recommended with G; not yet earned). |

| # | Decision |
|---|---|

As built: the business chooses code only (default) or code-or-password (Settings → Customer accounts). Default passwords are kept per business — hashed, single-use, valid 7 days — never on the WURK ID and never issued to someone whose WURK ID already has a password (they use their own). The first sign-in with one goes straight to "Set your own password" (WURK's password rules).

## 13.7 Payouts — specification (6 Nov 2026)

**Customer (portal → Payouts),** only when the business allows payouts (wallet rules): an amount between the business's minimum and their wallet, and bank details (account holder, bank, account number, branch code). The amount is **reserved at once** — it can't also be spent at checkout while the request waits. One open request at a time.

**Business (Customers → Payouts; `referrals.manage`):** requests oldest first, with the bank details to pay into. The business pays from its own bank, then **marks it paid with a reference** — or **declines with a reason**, which returns the amount to the wallet. The customer is emailed either way (wording in Settings → Messages).

**Bank details (POPIA — financial information):** stored encrypted; only the last four digits shown once the payout is paid or declined, when the full account number is deleted. Every step is logged, never with the account number. The ledger records the reservation as a payout (−), and a decline returns it (+), so wallets, statements and the deletion flow stay consistent.

## 13.8 My WURK — specification (7 Nov 2026)

**`my.wurk.co.za`** (configurable; its own surface and session policy; `my` is a reserved handle). Customers sign in with their **WURK ID** — code, or their own password — and see **one card per business** where they have a portal account: business name, wallet, rewards on the way, latest order or booking.

**Isolation:** each card is built inside that business's own context and data; nothing from one business is shown with, mixed into or totalled across another. A small **central index** records only *this WURK ID has an account at this business* (and whether they've hidden it) — no balances or details. It's added when they sign in to a business's portal, removed when a deletion is approved, and a one-off command indexes existing accounts.

**Open:** goes to that business's portal already signed in, through a **one-time pass** (signed, for that business and person, valid 60 seconds, usable once). Their POPIA consent with that business still applies as usual. **Remove from My WURK** hides a card; deleting information is still asked of the business (13.6e).

## 13.9 Reports — specification (7 Nov 2026)

**13.9a — Customers → Referral reports** (`referrals.manage`; packages with referrals), for a chosen period (this month, last 30 days, this year, all time):
- **Referral sales vs commission cost:** sales by referred customers (purchases where the buyer has a referrer), rewards earned in the period (net of reversals) split into own-purchase cashback and referral levels, and the cost as a share of those sales — by month.
- **Top referrers:** the ten customers whose referrals earned them the most (level 1+), with how many people they brought in the period.
- **Wallet liability (now):** available in wallets (owed if spent or paid out), on its way (pending), payouts waiting to be paid, and what expires in the next 30 days.
- **Payouts:** paid and declined in the period.
- **Dashboard tiles** on the business home: *Referral sales (this month)* and *Wallet liability* — through a new platform contract any module can use to add tiles (permission- and package-aware).

**13.9b — inter-branch settlement for wallets:** when a reward earned at one branch is spent at another, the 11.5 branch ledger records what the earning branch owes the spending branch; spends are matched to the branches where the rewards were earned, oldest first.
